Parking Crash Privacy Policy

About this policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you play Parking Crash, contact Tentacia, or visit this privacy-policy website. It describes the current game, which has no player accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, online leaderboards, or developer-operated cloud saves.

Information stored on your device

Parking Crash stores your current level, unlocked levels, tutorial completion, selected car skin and city theme, and music, sound-effect and vibration preferences on your device. These settings make the game work and remember your progress. The game does not upload this saved progress or these preferences to Tentacia.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or payment details to play.

Unity engine diagnostics

Parking Crash is built with Unity. Unity's engine diagnostics setting is currently enabled in the project. When diagnostics are active for a cloud-connected published build, Unity may collect technical information such as crash logs, runtime telemetry, configuration metadata, and device or application information. This information supports diagnosing problems and maintaining the game and engine's reliability. The specific collection depends on the Unity services and diagnostic configuration used for the build.

The game's separate Unity Analytics and advertising services are not enabled. Technical diagnostics should not be confused with advertising tracking. For details about Unity's handling of information, retention and available privacy requests, see Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity's diagnostic collection settings. You may also contact Tentacia with questions or requests concerning diagnostics associated with this game.

When you contact us

If you email tentaciahelp@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as a question, device details, or a screenshot. We use this information to respond, provide support, investigate reported issues, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Email is handled through our email service provider, Gmail.

We do not sell personal information or use support messages for advertising. Information may be processed by providers necessary to deliver support, or disclosed when required by law.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress and settings remain on your device until the app's data is removed. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app can retain that data. Device or iCloud backups may contain app data according to your Apple backup settings. Tentacia does not operate these backups and cannot remotely access or erase your local progress.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to respond, resolve an issue, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing us. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request. Information handled independently by Unity, Apple or GitHub is subject to their respective policies and privacy-request procedures.

You can change music, sound effects and vibration in the game's Settings. These controls affect gameplay preferences; they do not control Unity diagnostics.

Children and families

Players are not asked to submit personal information to use the game's features. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's information, including information voluntarily sent in a support message, can contact us to request assistance or deletion as appropriate.

Apple and this website

Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight and optional device-backup services independently. Information you provide to those services is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or third-party embedded media added by Tentacia. GitHub may process technical request information when you visit, such as your IP address and browser information, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website's hosting is separate from the game's local save data.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the game's information-handling practices change, and revise the effective date above. For privacy questions or requests, contact Tentacia at tentaciahelp@gmail.com.
